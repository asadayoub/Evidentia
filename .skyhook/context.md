# Project Context

## Problem Statement

Document-heavy teams must convert invoices, receipts, forms, purchase orders, shipping documents, and reports into reliable structured data. Extraction alone is insufficient: plausible but incorrect values, weak evidence, invisible corrections, changed revisions, lost authorization context, and duplicated delivery can make downstream records unsafe.

## Solution Overview

Evidentia owns the complete document-to-record lifecycle. It preserves original documents, produces schema-driven structured records, links values to source evidence, validates results, records human corrections and immutable revisions, obtains revision-bound authorization through a pluggable approval boundary, and delivers authorized records through recoverable destination adapters.

## Target Users

The initial benchmark centers on small accounting and operations teams processing recurring supplier invoices, supported by internal developers or automation agencies. The architecture supports additional document classes, languages, tenants, workflows, destinations, and application integrations through versioned schemas and adapters.

## Value Proposition

Turn documents into trustworthy business records whose source, interpretation, changes, validation, authorization, and delivery history can be inspected and reproduced.
